## Summary

1. **8 epochs buys about 30 s of pace on Map5 and costs reliability, with or without the height channel.** Over 3 seeds, E9L e8 finishes average 169.6 s and E1-8ep e8's 162–172 s, against 201.5 s for 4-epoch E1. But E9L finishes 13/36 and E1-8ep only 3/36, against 19/36 for E1 and 17/36 for the baseline. The height channel only shows its value at 8 epochs (13/36 vs 3/36, p ≈ 0.01).
2. **Map5 failures are spread out, not concentrated at a few corners.** For the chunk-head models, 89 failures fall at 48 different spots (50 m radius). The top spot holds 8%, and the top 10 together 49%. B01's first dirt corner is the one real hotspot (29% of B01 failures).
3. **The drift gap is distribution shift, not a missing technique.** The demos hold gas through 88% of drift-tap steps. On demo frames every model predicts the same (P(gas | brake) 0.90–0.95). In their own rollouts the baseline and E1 mostly brake without gas (median P(gas | brake) during their taps 0.00 and 0.11, from the logged probabilities; the sampled actions match). E9L gets closer (median 0.99, mean 0.65). Model taps lose 2–3 times the demos' speed. In their own states the models see an emergency brake, not a drift.
4. **Reversing out of crashes isn't in the demos.** Only one of 2,467 training runs reverses mid-run, for 0.5 s. Five more drive backwards from the start (gimmick maps). E9L's reversing most likely comes from those.
5. **ser5 can't run evals on its own CPU.** Under game load it manages 5.3 steps/s per lane, against 19.3 with the laptop GPU. The per-rollout wall-time cap (120 s) then cuts most rollouts short.
6. **corpus3 is harvested: 2,500 new keyboard replays and their maps,** 30.1 h of driving (corpus2 is 28.8 h). Every quota filled: Tech 875, SpeedTech 625, Race 500, Offroad 300, FullSpeed 200. That took 10,954 requests at 1/s over 5.2 h, using `harvest-quota` (commit 491b05b). It is not collected yet.

## Where rollouts fail

All valid Map5 and B01 rollouts with positions in their step logs, from 14 chunk-head evals (E1, E9 and E9L, every seed). The crash point is the last position at or above 60 km/h before the rollout ended; points within 50 m are grouped.

- **Map5:** 89 of 151 rollouts failed, at 48 different spots. The most common spot holds 8%, and the top 10 together 49%. By section, CP4 → CP5 is the worst (33% of failures). 87% of failures end as `stationary`: the car is stuck after a crash, not slow.
- **B01:** 78 of 128 failed, at 32 spots. The first dirt corner (two neighbouring groups, about 5–7 s in, before CP1) holds 29%. The rest is spread out.
- Pooled over every model, including the older ones: Map5 has 71 spots for 264 failures, and B01's first corner holds 47% of its failures. The newer models mostly get past that corner.

**So Map5 losses come from many small mistakes all along the track, not a few hard corners.** Fixing corners one at a time won't do much. What helps is training data that covers states just off the demo line. The analysis was a one-off and is not in the repo. It can be redone from each eval's `rollouts.jsonl` and `steps/`.

## Drifting: the models hold gas in demo states but not in their own

A tap here is brake turning on while steering above 150 km/h, as in `drift_rate.py`. The 1 s columns are measured from the tap's onset.

| | taps | entry speed | tap length (steps) | gas held during tap | sliding in next 1 s | speed lost in 1 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| demos (500 runs) | 2,093 | 291 km/h | 7 | **88%** | **78%** | 39 km/h |
| baseline | 227 | 196 | 5 | 20% | 9% | 73 |
| E1 | 294 | 205 | 6 | 32% | 15% | 80 |
| E9 | 296 | 198 | 9 | 28% | 19% | 87 |
| E9L e8 | 282 | 228 | 10 | 49% | 37% | 74 |

- **Speed doesn't explain it.** By entry-speed band (150–200, 200–250, 250–300, 300+ km/h), the demos hold gas in 85–90% of tap steps in every band. The models hold it in 17–58%.
- **On demo frames the models agree with the demos.** `offline_check.py` now reports P(gas | brake) at the demo's taps: baseline 0.925, E1 0.929, E9 0.908, E9L e8 0.947 (commit 8c8aede).
- **In their own rollouts they don't.** The step logs carry the model's probabilities. At the models' own tap steps, median P(gas | brake) is 0.00 for the baseline, 0.11 for E1 and 0.99 for E9L. The sampled actions match these, so sampling isn't the cause.
- **So the drift gap is distribution shift too.** The models reach states (entry speed, line, yaw) where their best guess is an emergency brake. That fits the spread-out failures above.

## Reversing out of crashes

`reverse_rate.py`'s definition (brake held without gas, forward speed below −1 m/s, for at least 0.5 s), applied to corpus2's `samples.jsonl`:

- **Runs kept for training (2,467):** 7 runs contain a spell, 0.1% of frames. Five start reversing at 0.1 s and drive backwards for 3–49 s at up to 376 km/h; these are backwards-driving gimmick maps. One is a genuine mid-run spell: 0.5 s at 28 km/h. One is a 0.6 s spell at the start.
- **Runs dropped for respawns (16):** 2 runs, 5 spells, including a 10.7 s recovery.

There are almost no crash recoveries to imitate. E9L's reversing most likely comes from the gimmick maps' "hold brake to go backwards", applied when it is stuck against a wall.

## New corpus harvest (corpus3)

`tmnf-collect harvest-quota` (commit 491b05b) fills per-tag quotas from the top of TMX by awards:

- **It stops** when every quota is full, awards fall below `--min-awards` (5), or `--max-scanned` maps have been read (25,000). It never walks the whole site.
- **It skips** maps already collected (`--have`, the 2,525 maps in ser5's `Challenges/tmnf-collect`) and maps already in its manifest, so it resumes.
- **Per map:** the leaderboard, then the map, then up to 3 runs near the median, until one is a keyboard run on the current map version. Leaderboards keep runs from older versions of a map, which can't be re-driven (6 of the first 13 maps tried). Pad and old-version runs go to `corpus3.rejected/<kind>`, and a map with no usable run is deleted again.
- **Courtesy:** one request at a time, 1 s apart. A 429 or 5xx waits 60 s, doubling. 10 errors in a row stop it.

**Quotas.** These are TMX's tags; "Race" is TMX's default for an untagged map. The braking and sliding profiles come from corpus2's demos:

| tag | corpus2 maps | brake share | slide share >100 km/h | corpus3 quota |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Race (untagged) | 1,186 | 5.1% | 22.6% | 500 |
| FullSpeed | 665 | 0.3% | 17.2% | 200 |
| Tech | 353 | **9.7%** | 26.7% | **875** |
| SpeedTech | 170 | 7.2% | 26.3% | **625** |
| Offroad | 105 | 0.9% | **48.5%** | 300 |

A dry run (search only, 164 requests) filled every quota within the top 16,348 maps by awards, at awards 8 and above. Downloads go deeper, because about 30% of median runs are on a pad.
